The enterprise ontology and `cmd.ttl` are semantic authority. Generated JSON, Markdown, Rust, receipts, and verifier reports are consequences. No generated surface may self-award external certification, contract, or enterprise standing.

```text

```

Internal and external candidates remain inert. The pure kernel may manufacture plans and typed intents only. External actuation requires BRCE, an exact grant, independent consequence observation, and causal receipts.

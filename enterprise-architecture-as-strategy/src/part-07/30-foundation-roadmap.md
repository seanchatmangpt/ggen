# 30. GGEN-EA-000 to GGEN-EA-030: Foundation

## GGEN-EA-000 - Enterprise Constitution

Deliver:

- enterprise boundary;
- architecture principles;
- authority model;
- standing vocabulary;
- exception lifecycle;
- root receipt vocabulary.

Positive witness: an architecture initiative is admitted or refused under the constitution.

## GGEN-EA-010 - Enterprise Metamodel

Deliver:

- capability;
- value stream;
- product;
- service;
- repository;
- component;
- ontology;
- pack;
- projection;
- transition;
- evidence;
- lifecycle.

Positive witness: one real ggen capability is represented across strategy, implementation, and evidence.

## GGEN-EA-020 - Federated Architecture Repository

Deliver:

- source adapters;
- provenance;
- identity resolution;
- graph query surface;
- snapshot and replay.

Positive witness: repository, pack, ontology, and receipt data are joined without losing source identity.

## GGEN-EA-030 - Capability and Value-Stream Map

Deliver:

- lawful manufacturing value stream;
- capability ownership;
- component realization;
- consumer relationships;
- orphan and duplication detection.

Positive witness: the system identifies one duplicated or unsupported capability relation and explains it.

The foundation wave ends when the architecture graph is useful without yet performing broad automation. It should answer "what exists, what it means, who owns it, and what evidence supports it."

# 31. GGEN-EA-040 to GGEN-EA-060: Lifecycle and Contracts

## GGEN-EA-040 - Ontology Lifecycle

Deliver identity, qualification, admission, composition, capacity, compatibility, deprecation, retirement, and archive states.

Positive witness: two ontology versions produce a semantic diff, migration recommendation, and retirement decision.

## GGEN-EA-050 - Pack and Product Lifecycle

Deliver constituent and distribution roles, promotion, support, compatibility, consumer evidence, deprecation, and retirement.

Positive witness: an internal pack is promoted to a bounded distribution pack with independent replay.

## GGEN-EA-060 - Architecture Contracts

Deliver contract vocabulary, requirement linkage, quality attributes, authority, capacity, evidence, exceptions, and acceptance gates.

Positive witness: a contract generates human review, CI obligations, and a machine compliance result.

This wave closes the primary object lifecycles. The ecosystem should no longer treat a file path, repository, or published package as sufficient identity or standing.

The user-visible result is `ggen architecture doctor` with lifecycle-aware explanations.

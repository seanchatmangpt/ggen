# 32. GGEN-EA-070 to GGEN-EA-090: Transition and Operations

## GGEN-EA-070 - Transition Architecture

Deliver baseline, target, transition states, MFW/POWL/PDDL projections, work packages, dependencies, rollback, and plan certificates.

Positive witness: a synthetic enterprise migration produces an executable partial order and bounded rollback plan.

## GGEN-EA-080 - Compliance and Promotion

Deliver universal Gall checkpoints, compliance packets, exception processing, promotion receipts, and refusal explanations.

Positive witness: a nonconforming implementation is refused before promotion and the exact missing evidence is named.

## GGEN-EA-090 - Operational Architecture

Deliver capacity envelopes, observability projections, OCEL mappings, drift detection, product telemetry, incident-to-change linkage, and retirement triggers.

Positive witness: an observed performance regression changes the architecture plan without silently mutating the target state.

This wave connects the architecture repository to operating reality. It must preserve privilege separation: observation and planning may be broad, but actuation remains capability-bounded through BRCE.

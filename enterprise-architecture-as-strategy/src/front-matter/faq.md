# Working-Backwards FAQ

## Is ggen replacing enterprise architects?

No. Architects continue to define outcomes, principles, operating models, boundaries, risk posture, target states, exceptions, and organizational choices. ggen turns admitted decisions into traceable consequences and evidence.

## Is this another configuration management database?

No. Inventory is one function. The architecture graph also represents requirements, constraints, derivations, capabilities, candidate solutions, transition states, authority, evidence, compatibility, and lifecycle.

## Is ggen a deployment engine?

No. ggen constructs artifacts and bounded intents. BRCE or another admitted broker controls actuation.

## Is the architecture graph one universal ontology?

No. The system composes foundation, common, industry, organization, and consumer ontologies through explicit profiles and contracts.

## How does this differ from model-driven engineering?

The projection space includes not only application code but infrastructure, policy, tests, proofs, observability, planning, evidence, migration, and retirement. It also governs admission, authority, standing, and replay.

## How does this differ from infrastructure as code?

Infrastructure code becomes one projection of richer knowledge. The same fact can generate policy, monitoring, recovery, tests, and compliance evidence.

## Can TOGAF and ArchiMate still be used?

Yes. TOGAF supplies the architecture-development method. ArchiMate is a stakeholder-view and exchange projection. Neither needs to become the canonical internal source.

## What is the main metric?

Validated knowledge leverage: how many useful, owned, and evidenced consequences are produced per independently maintained semantic assertion.

## What is the main safety boundary?

No consequence may outrank its admitted knowledge, granted authority, or observed evidence.

## What is the first useful product?

A lifecycle-aware architecture repository and `ggen architecture doctor`, followed by impact analysis and transition planning.

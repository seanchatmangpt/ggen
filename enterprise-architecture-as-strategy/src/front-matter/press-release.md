# Working-Backwards Press Release

## ggen Launches Enterprise Architecture as Strategy

**PASADENA, Calif. - July 30, 2026** - ggen today announced Enterprise Architecture as Strategy, a capability that allows organizations to represent enterprise strategy, operating models, ontologies, requirements, policies, capabilities, products, applications, technologies, and transition states as governed semantic knowledge and then manufacture their lawful operational consequences.

Traditional enterprise architecture is distributed across diagrams, documents, spreadsheets, standards, tickets, repositories, infrastructure code, dashboards, and institutional memory. Each representation can drift independently. Enterprise Architecture as Strategy replaces this fragmentation with a canonical architecture graph connected to deterministic manufacturing, planning, governance, actuation, process evidence, and replay.

Organizations can use the same admitted architectural fact to generate software, infrastructure, APIs, schemas, policies, tests, proof obligations, observability, migration plans, compliance reports, and receipts. The architecture does not execute with ambient authority. Generated intents cross an explicit broker boundary, and material claims receive standing only after validation and observation.

"Strategy normally loses precision as it moves toward implementation," said Sean Chatman, creator of ggen. "ggen makes architectural meaning the reusable production asset. Strategy no longer sits above execution. It becomes the governed knowledge from which implementation and evidence are manufactured."

The capability includes a federated architecture repository, ontology and pack lifecycle, capability and value-stream mapping, architecture contracts, baseline and target states, transition planning, impact analysis, capacity envelopes, Gall compliance gates, BRCE actuation boundaries, OCEL process evidence, and generated stakeholder views.

The initial developer experience centers on `ggen architecture doctor`, which explains what the architecture believes, why an ontology or pack is present, who owns it, what a proposed change affects, what it will cost, which evidence is missing, and what the next lawful transition can be.

Enterprise Architecture as Strategy is delivered incrementally through a Gall roadmap beginning with the enterprise constitution and metamodel, continuing through lifecycle, contracts, transition architecture, compliance, operations, and portfolio management, and ending in self-hosting. At the crown checkpoint, a material change can be traced from strategy through capability, product, ontology, pack, implementation, execution, observation, and receipt before actuation.

The long-term objective is an enterprise capable of explaining, manufacturing, governing, observing, and proving its own change.

# 13. Packs, Constituents, Distributions, and the Marketplace

A ggen pack is a portable manufacturing law.

A strong pack contains:

$$
Pack =
SemanticKernel
+ Operators
+ Projections
+ Contract
+ Lifecycle
+ Evidence
$$

The semantic kernel defines meaning, constraints, and imported authority. Operators include queries, constructors, planners, validators, and refusal rules. Projections define artifact families. The contract defines inputs, outputs, capabilities, write scope, compatibility, and receipts.

Two pack roles are especially important.

## Constituent Pack

A constituent pack participates inside a larger manufacturing boundary. It may depend on host-local conventions and private knowledge. Its standing is local to that host.

## Distribution Pack

A distribution pack crosses a repository, organization, product, or trust boundary. It must close or expose dependencies, reduce hidden authority, publish compatibility law, ship conformance evidence, and support independent consumer admission.

Internal and external are relational classifications. A distribution pack admitted by a consumer may become an internal constituent of that consumer's system, but its authority must not increase automatically.

The marketplace should therefore operate as an Architecture Building Block Exchange, not a file catalogue. Search and ranking should include:

- capability;
- semantic coverage;
- compatibility;
- lifecycle;
- performance envelope;
- authority requested;
- proof depth;
- consumer evidence;
- replacement relations;
- maintenance standing.

Promotion from constituent to distribution pack is a governed state transition:

```text
dependency closure
-> contract extraction
-> authority reduction
-> compatibility declaration
-> conformance suite
-> promotion receipt
```

The marketplace can then support lawful substitution. Two packs may be observationally equivalent under a published contract even when their internal templates, languages, or engines differ.

This turns the external ecosystem into a semantic economy. Producers publish stable meaning and manufacturing operators. Consumers combine them with local context to produce local artifact families without surrendering sovereignty.

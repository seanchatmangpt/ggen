# 41. Conclusion: The Enterprise That Can Prove Its Change

Enterprise architecture becomes strategy when it governs how the enterprise repeatedly creates value, not when it produces a persuasive target-state document.

ggen provides the manufacturing mechanism. Ontologies preserve meaning. Packs distribute lawful operators. Graphlaw admits and derives semantic state. star-toml carries bounded configuration. CMD explores candidates. MFW, POWL, and PDDL plan transitions. mfact and procint provide formal assurance. Gall establishes evidentiary standing. BRCE bounds actuation. wasm4pm observes process. cargo-cicd governs release. Receipts preserve lineage and replay.

No component is sufficient alone.

The target enterprise possesses a canonical architecture graph that connects strategy, operating model, capability, product, information, application, technology, lifecycle, transition, authority, and evidence.

From that graph, it can manufacture:

- architecture views;
- software and infrastructure;
- policy;
- plans;
- tests;
- proof obligations;
- observability;
- governance packets;
- migration;
- retirement;
- receipts.

The enterprise remains open to reality because observed consequence returns as evidence and may change the architecture.

The final law is:

> The architecture may generate every lawful consequence of admitted knowledge, but no consequence may outrank its authority or evidence.

This is Enterprise Architecture as Strategy with ggen: not architecture above execution, and not execution without architecture, but one governed system in which strategy can survive translation, implementation can remain explainable, and change can be proven.

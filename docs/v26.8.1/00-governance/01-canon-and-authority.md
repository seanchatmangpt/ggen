## Purpose

This document defines the authority hierarchy for ggen v26.8.1 and the ggen-legacy sunset. Repository constitutions, admitted ontologies, exact source revisions, executable verifiers, and cryptographic receipts outrank prose summaries.

- ggen manufactures artifacts from admitted knowledge graphs through Resolve, Enrich, Extract, Render, Write, and Receipt.

- Generated surfaces are edited through their authority source, not by hand.

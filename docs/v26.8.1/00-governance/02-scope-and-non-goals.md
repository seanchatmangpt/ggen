# Scope and non-goals

## In scope

The v26.8.1 research program covers every active workspace package, CLI noun and verb, LSP diagnostic, configuration schema, ontology and graph engine, Tera projection path, filesystem write mode, receipt and replay surface, telemetry and OCEL emission path, marketplace pack, bblock composition, self-hosting control plane, CI verifier, release-law actuator, benchmark, and historical ggen-legacy capability.

## Non-goals

- No prose document may promote a capability to ALIVE.
- No benchmark estimate may be represented as an integrated ggen result without an executed pipeline receipt.
- No external cloud, deployment, registry, or infrastructure actuation is assigned to ggen when BRCE owns the DO boundary.
- No process-mining analysis is imported into ggen; ggen emits process evidence while wasm4pm owns discovery and conformance.
- No legacy surface is deleted merely because it appears redundant.

## Completion boundary

The research corpus is complete only when all discovered surfaces are represented in the machine-readable coverage matrix and all UNKNOWN entries are either resolved or explicitly accepted as sunset blockers.

# Risk register

## Critical risks

1. **False completeness:** 80+ documents could create the appearance of coverage without mapping actual repository surfaces.
2. **Authority drift:** generated documentation, active code, ontology, and historical specifications may disagree.
3. **Verifier substitution:** source inspection may be mistaken for observed behavior.
4. **Legacy omission:** obscure aliases, schema fields, receipt versions, or recovery paths may disappear silently.
5. **Benchmark category error:** Tera or Oxigraph microbenchmarks may be represented as integrated manufacturing throughput.
6. **Self-witnessing:** a subsystem may incorrectly promote its own evidence to ALIVE.
7. **Boundary violation:** process analysis or external actuation may migrate into ggen despite explicit ownership rules.
8. **Coordination duplication:** command surfaces may retain parallel identity, resolution, lockfile, receipt, or standing implementations.
9. **Publication regression:** workspace restructuring may affect the public root package identity.
10. **Recovery failure:** legacy code may be archived without a tested restoration and replay path.

Every risk requires an owner, falsifier, detection command, mitigation, residual standing, and sunset consequence in the final machine-readable report.

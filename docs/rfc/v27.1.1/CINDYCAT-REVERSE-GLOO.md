## 1. What “Reverse Gloo” means

Gloo aggregated church tech into a platform that sits **between** the flock and the tools — custody, bundling, take-rate. CindyCat reverses that axis:

| Axis | Gloo (platform default) | CindyCat (Reverse Gloo) |
|------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Who holds the relationship | Platform / marketplace | Woman-owner + local body (patronage) |
| Money path | SaaS seat / bundle / take-rate | Third-party payer → acknowledged gift / cell — **0% custody** |
| Product center | Church-ops software stack | Warm presence brand (Julia Veach warmth) over a receipted manufacture spine |
| Trust signal | Feature catalog + integrations | Zero custody + named acknowledgment + unsentimental deal math |
| Failure mode | Lock-in / intermediary opacity | Soft brand without a closed Event #1 non-negotiable → **REFUSED** (no Event) |
| Follow-on | More seats inside the platform | Fourth Watch as **natural** cyber follow-on — never cold open |

CindyCat is the **Jobs** face: warmth, patronage, owner narrative. The manufacture spine (ggen → Taffy → CASTLE → Smalt → revops) is the **Woz** floor: receipts, courts, refusal. Do not swap them.

Event #1 does **not** open until all three are explicit (written acknowledgment from the counterparty or their admitted payer). Missing any one → **REFUSED_EVENT_1**.

| # | Non-negotiable | Pass criterion | Fail standing |

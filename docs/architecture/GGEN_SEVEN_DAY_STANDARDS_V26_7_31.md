# ggen Seven-Day Standards Profile v26.7.31

## Preserve

This profile admits the standards developed and executed across the ggen programme from 2026-07-23 through 2026-07-30.

It is not a changelog and it does not convert every recent branch into established standing. It is a deterministic certification input with 18 named checkpoints. Each checkpoint carries one authority layer, one admission status, one enforceable law, and one material falsifier.

Stable identity:

```text
GGEN-STANDARDS-7D-2026-07-30
```

The profile is bound by canonical BLAKE3 digest into:

- every certification requirement receipt;
- every cumulative certification award;
- every generated TAI rebuild roadmap;
- every successful TAI rebuild receipt;
- the machine-readable standards verifier report.

Evidence produced against an earlier standards identity or digest is refused rather than silently upgraded.

## Fence

The profile separates three states:

| Status | Meaning | May contribute to bounded certification? |
|---|---|---:|
| `ADMITTED` | enforced by merged or exact-head executable repository law | yes |
| `INHERITED` | preserved as an independently receipted execution rail | yes |
| `PENDING_CHECKPOINT` | explicit future Gall checkpoint without complete execution standing | no |

The profile currently contains:

```text
13 ADMITTED
4 INHERITED
1 PENDING_CHECKPOINT
18 TOTAL
```

`STD-18-CI-CONTROL-PLANE-G0` remains pending. Its inventory and ownership law is preserved, but it cannot inflate the bounded certification crown before its own exact-head evidence exists.

## Calculus

```text
A = μ(O*)
R = receipt(A)
```

The complete operation law is:

```text
O → O* → I → G → A → R → O′
```

- `O` — partial or stale observation;
- `O*` — admitted, aligned, complete, grounded, and bounded observation;
- `I` — deterministic intent;
- `G` — exact authority grant;
- `A` — bounded consequence;
- `R` — causal receipt;
- `O′` — independently re-observed postcondition.

Command success is not consequence success. A generated artifact is not authority. A verified candidate is not an actuation grant.

## The 18 checkpoints

### STD-01 — Admitted observation

**Authority:** Constitutional  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Only `O*` enters manufacture. Partial, stale, unbounded, or unaudited observation cannot silently acquire standing.

**Falsifier:** manufacture from an observation whose identity, provenance, boundary, exclusions, or freshness is absent.

### STD-02 — Receipted consequence

**Authority:** Constitutional  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Zero unreceipted actuation. A successful command must be followed by consequence observation and receipt.

**Falsifier:** promote from exit status while the promised state or artifact is absent.

### STD-03 — State and authority separation

**Authority:** Constitutional  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

```text
candidate != verified != authorized != actuated
planning != actuation
cognition != authority
```

**Falsifier:** allow a plan, model output, generated file, or verifier result to execute directly.

### STD-04 — BRCE-only DO

**Authority:** Execution  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

BRCE is the sole lawful external actuation boundary. Architecture kernels, planners, hooks, and model integrations manufacture bounded intents only.

**Falsifier:** import or invoke filesystem, process, network, cloud, deployment, or production actuators from a pure kernel.

### STD-05 — Typed standing

**Authority:** Verification  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Standing derives from observed execution within a declared boundary. Metadata, lifecycle, documentation, badges, and synthetic evidence cannot self-promote.

**Falsifier:** treat `DEPRECATED`, `RETIRED`, a certificate, or source presence as `ALIVE`.

### STD-06 — Ontology authority

**Authority:** Semantic  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Public-ontology-first graphs own domain meaning. Prose, generated code, trackers, reports, and badges are projections or observations.

**Falsifier:** hand-edit a generated consequence or introduce a competing private vocabulary where an admitted public semantic basis exists.

### STD-07 — ggen manufacturing law

**Authority:** Manufacturing  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

```text
Resolve → Enrich → Extract → Render → Write → Receipt
```

Every output has one owner. Projection cardinality, paths, bodies, hooks, shapes, resources, and ownership state are admitted before actuation.

**Falsifier:** duplicate canonical writers, unsafe aliases, undeclared targets, unbounded rows, or post-hook feasibility checks.

### STD-08 — Determinism and replay

**Authority:** Manufacturing  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Repeated manufacture from the same admitted graph must be byte-identical and independently replayable.

**Falsifier:** first and second sync differ, receipt recomputation differs, or replay depends on mutable ambient state.

### STD-09 — Part Passport

**Authority:** Semantic  
**Status:** `INHERITED`

An interchangeable realization is bound by conditioned inputs, guaranteed outputs, causal polarity, authority, resources, isolation, host and jurisdiction profiles, conformity, evidence, support, replacement, and retirement.

**Falsifier:** approve substitution from syntax or ABI compatibility alone.

### STD-10 — Gall checkpoints

**Authority:** Delivery  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Complex systems advance through dependency-closed checkpoints with obligations, exclusions, witnesses, falsifiers, receipts, replay, and explicit crown closure.

**Falsifier:** claim crown completeness from a partial checkpoint or delete a Chesterton fence without replacement evidence.

### STD-11 — Five evidence surfaces

**Authority:** Evidence  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Every certification obligation requires:

```text
positive witness
+ negative falsifier
+ independent verifier
+ receipt verifier
+ deterministic replay
```

**Falsifier:** any surface is absent, empty, self-produced without segregation, or detached from the exact artifact.

### STD-12 — Real-boundary testing

**Authority:** Verification  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Chicago-style verification crosses real process, filesystem, state, protocol, or telemetry boundaries and externalizes causal evidence. Primary evidence paths cannot be mocked or fabricated.

**Falsifier:** mocks, stubs, hard-coded success, synthetic telemetry, fake receipts, or internal-state assertions substituted for consequence.

### STD-13 — Exact-head execution

**Authority:** Verification  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

A claim binds the exact commit/tree, toolchain, policy, inputs, outputs, and verifier report.

**Falsifier:** reuse an earlier-head receipt, infer execution from a queued shell, or call an `action_required` workflow green.

### STD-14 — Process evidence

**Authority:** Evidence  
**Status:** `INHERITED`

Execution emits causally linked OCEL/process evidence, predecessor receipts, postcondition observation, and replay.

**Falsifier:** hand-construct process evidence, infer causality from timestamps, or prove only final-state coincidence while trajectories differ.

### STD-15 — POWL and MFW boundary

**Authority:** Planning  
**Status:** `INHERITED`

POWL v2 and MFW own admitted lifecycle and planning semantics. ggen projects them. Motion Packets and plans remain intents until BRCE grants execution.

**Falsifier:** permit a planner, serializer, or generated transition to self-authorize.

### STD-16 — Formal proof discipline

**Authority:** Verification  
**Status:** `INHERITED`

Proof-relevant Lean values inhabit `Type` and are definitions. Theorem interfaces must be non-circular, non-vacuous, and observation-complete. Identity-keyed time and full trajectories are preserved when semantically relevant.

**Falsifier:** Type-valued theorem declarations, conclusion-as-premise, empty native relations, positional timestamp aliasing, or erased intermediate states.

### STD-17 — Bounded automatic and autonomic operation

**Authority:** Execution  
**Status:** `ADMITTED`

Automatic routing and MAPE-K repair are finite, idempotent, retry-bounded, circuit-broken, error-budgeted, Andon-governed, rollback-capable, and receipted through one authority rail.

**Falsifier:** emergency repair, retry, rollback, or promotion bypasses admission, budgets, receipts, or consequence verification.

### STD-18 — CI control-plane G0

**Authority:** Delivery  
**Status:** `PENDING_CHECKPOINT`

CI G0 inventories every workflow, assigns one semantic owner per production output, measures trigger fan-out, and preserves Chesterton retirement fences before consolidation.

**Falsifier:** delete or merge workflows before exact inventory, missing-owner, duplicate-authority, trigger, and output-ownership checks execute.

This checkpoint remains visible and non-promoting.

## Exclusions

The profile does not claim:

- that every open PR from the seven-day window is merged;
- that CI G0 is complete;
- that inherited MFW, wasm4pm, OCEL, Lean, Part Passport, or external service rails are universally production-ready;
- that synthetic or bounded reference evidence proves production standing;
- that an external certificate proves internal capability;
- that a standards document outranks executable law.

## Falsifier boundary

The combined certification must refuse:

- incorrect profile identity or version;
- changed profile bytes with a stale digest;
- a requirement receipt bound to another profile;
- a TAI target bound to another profile;
- pending checkpoints counted as promoting evidence;
- direct actuation outside BRCE;
- generated output above graph authority;
- first/second-sync drift;
- mocks, stubs, fake receipts, or fabricated telemetry;
- exact-head ancestry detached from the current TAI parent.

# CertificationAssist v26.7.30

## Purpose

CertificationAssist converts credential bodies of knowledge into evidence-backed internal capability and reusable ggen manufacturing assets.

The certificate is an external receipt. It does not automatically establish internal standing.

## Architecture

```text
Credential Horizon
→ admitted objectives and competency graph
→ Triple Darkness analysis
→ dependency-closed 80/20 plan
→ manufactured probes and whiteboard projection
→ deterministic Candidate/Examiner/Adjudicator self-play
→ bounded lab or simulation
→ evidence admission
→ standing proposal
→ human-authorized promotion
→ BLAKE3 receipt and replay
```

## Pack structure

```text
packs/certification-assist-pack/
├── ontology.ttl
├── gates/
├── queries/
├── templates/
└── consumer/certification-assist/   # manufactured in verification
```

The first pack composes four reusable capability families:

1. **Assist kernel** — observations, phases, probes, self-play, evidence, refusals and receipts.
2. **Capability standing** — internal standing lattice, external credential separation and promotion law.
3. **Whiteboard projection** — `DRAW NEXT`, `SAY NEXT`, `WHY`, `TRADEOFF`, `WATCH FOR`.
4. **Certification profile** — credential objectives, priority factors and systems-architecture speedrun.

## Public semantic basis

- CTDL represents credentials and learning opportunities.
- CTDL-ASN represents competencies and prerequisite structure.
- PROV-O represents evidence production and derivation.
- SKOS represents knowledge concepts and horizon gaps.
- Chatman vocabulary represents Triple Darkness, internal standing, whiteboard projections, self-play and evidence-gated promotion.

Public vocabularies are reused where their semantics fit. Project-specific constitutional terms remain in the Chatman namespace rather than being forced into unrelated public predicates.

## MCP and A2A

MCP exposes:

- Skill Twin, Credential Horizon and standing-receipt resources;
- gap diagnosis, speedrun planning, probe manufacture, evidence evaluation and promotion-proposal tools.

The promotion proposal is approval-gated. MCP task augmentation is not declared because the 2025-11-25 task facility is experimental.

A2A owns stateful multi-agent work:

- Candidate Twin;
- Examiner Twin;
- Domain Adjudicator;
- tasks, messages, parts and artifacts;
- bounded self-play and adjudication.

## Automation

The deterministic planner ranks objectives by:

```text
interview frequency
× ecosystem dependency
× transfer breadth
× gatekeeping value
× Triple Darkness risk
──────────────────────────────────────────────
learning cost × (existing coverage + 1)
```

Selection remains prerequisite-closed. A high score cannot bypass a required objective.

## Autonomics

The autonomic learning controller implements a bounded MAPE-K loop:

```text
Monitor   current standing and evidence
Analyze   highest-leverage unresolved gap
Plan      one bounded practice intent
Execute   deterministic simulation or lab through an authorized rail
Knowledge record the evidence candidate and receipts
```

The loop has a hard eight-cycle ceiling. It cannot directly actuate external systems and cannot promote standing.

## Self-play

The first deterministic self-play fixture uses:

- Candidate Twin to expose current standing and produce an answer;
- Examiner Twin to apply the target and adversarial follow-up;
- Domain Adjudicator to compare the response with the admitted objective and emit an artifact.

The simulation is not itself promotion evidence unless an explicit crown declares and verifies that surface.

## Automatic operating rail

The generated consumer reuses the automatic/autonomic machinery introduced by the parent stack:

```text
trigger admission
→ deterministic route
→ manufactured intent
→ exact grant
→ atomic actuation and receipt
→ consequence observation
→ causal receipt chain
```

There is no second broker and no emergency execution path.

## First crown

The release is conjunctive across 12 capabilities and three proof surfaces:

```text
12 × (positive execution + negative refusal + receipt/replay) = 36 obligations
```

The first systems fixture contains eight objectives and one Triple Darkness dependency witness for fencing tokens.

## Standing

The source pack and verifier workflow are implementation artifacts. They do not establish production learning efficacy, psychometric validity, universal system-design coverage, issuer recognition, or completed external certification.

`ALIVE` may be assigned only to the bounded generated crown after exact-head execution. Aggregate product standing remains `PARTIAL_ALIVE` until real learner evidence, external credential receipts and repeated interview-transfer results are admitted.

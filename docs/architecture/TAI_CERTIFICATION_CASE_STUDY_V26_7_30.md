# TAI Certification Case Study v26.7.30

## Preserve

TAI is the first complete enterprise reconstruction scenario for the ggen Building Blocks certification program.

It preserves three non-equivalent objects:

1. **Historical TAI** — the observed systems-of-systems enterprise and Total Quality Leadership tradition.
2. **Automated Technical Capability Company** — the target enterprise whose product is mission capability.
3. **Target Architecture Instance** — the exact versioned graph object a candidate reconstructs and verifies.

Adjacency is not equivalence. Historical success does not imply current standing. A target-enterprise description is not an admitted instance. An admitted instance is not `ALIVE` without execution evidence.

Private constitutional terms remain explicit rather than being forced into unrelated public predicates.

### Manufacturing

`ggen` is the compiler between admitted enterprise knowledge and lawful architecture, software, infrastructure, policy, workflow, evidence, and documentation projections.

Generated files are projections. They do not outrank the graph that manufactured them.

### Planning and transition architecture

MFW and POWL v2 provide dependency-closed transition planning below the BRCE constitutional boundary.

They may determine enabled bounded transitions. They do not decide arbitrary program safety and do not grant their own authority.

### Actuation

BRCE owns the only `DO` path.

```text

→ manufactured intent
→ exact grant

→ consequence observation

```

### Process evidence

wasm4pm and OCEL represent execution as replayable process evidence:

```text

→ admitted state
→ authorized transformation
→ verified consequence
→ receipted event
```

Failures such as timeout, unsupported operation, or tool failure remain typed observations. They do not collapse into truth.

```text

→ chaos
→ stress
→ benchmark
→ independent verifier report
```

Lean, typed systems, deterministic fixtures, LSP diagnostics, and independent replay may supply different proof surfaces. No single checkpoint is silently promoted to crown standing.

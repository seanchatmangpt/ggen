Its canonical pipeline is:

```text
MCP/A2A exhaust
→ OCEL/process evidence
→ POWL v2 route and concurrency law
```

LLMs may propose observations, questions, ontologies, plans, templates, or candidate artifacts. They never determine standing, release authority, execution authority, or settlement.

## Universal operational nouns

### Doctor

Doctor observes and diagnoses. It binds environment state, root-cause graphs, policy divergence, missing evidence, and deterministic remediation plans. Doctor never actuates.

Wizard may select, parameterize, plan, and verify scaffolds. It must never blindly generate hot law.

### Truthforge

Truthforge is the admission boundary. It evaluates claims, proof correspondence, negative controls, independent acceptance, and replay. It returns the complete lawful outcome lattice, not only YES or NO:


### Telco

Telco supplies universal communication and coordination surfaces:

- **office** — bounded administrative and capability namespace;
- **register** — discoverable identity, contract, standing, and location;
- **line** — typed point-to-point request/result channel;
- **bridge** — protocol and trust-domain translation across CLI, MCP, A2A, local, edge, fog, and cloud;
- **record** — immutable exhaust, receipt, replay, and settlement evidence.

MCP and A2A are Telco surfaces. POWL v2 coordinates work across those surfaces.

- ontology modules;

- languages;
- jurisdictions;
- organization profiles;
- runtimes.

The product of these axes is the SBB distribution context count. Vision 2030 aggregates only externally accepted SBB reports.

```text
canonical_units = Σ accepted commit-equivalent units
delivered_instances = Σ accepted delivered capability instances
```

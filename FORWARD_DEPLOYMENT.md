## Local role

Within that portfolio, `ggen` is the ontology-to-artifact projection engine. It turns admitted semantic graphs, queries, and templates into deterministic local artifacts for applications, infrastructure, protocols, documentation, tests, and release surfaces.

`ggen` **renders**. Rendering does not by itself establish truth, authority, successful execution, or production standing. Generated artifacts must still cross their formal admission, runtime verification, actuation, receipt, and replay boundaries.

```text
admitted graph → query → template → generated artifact
→ consumer build/execution → verification → receipt → standing
```
